# Retired c-Si PV panel: characterization, custody and supervised validation plan

**Prepared:** 19 November 2026. **Status:** planning template only. No panel or fraction was received or processed.

## Objective and boundary

Define a traceable route to characterize a retired crystalline-silicon module and, only if an equipped host approves, compare separation candidates. This is not an operating procedure and authorizes no handling, electrical test, cutting, heating, grinding, chemical work or shipment. The project currently verifies zero physical samples.

## Supervised validation gates

### Gate 0 — acceptance and permission
Obtain written owner permission, supervisor, receiving-host approval, packaging/transport instructions, risk review, analytical plan and residue route. If any item is missing, no physical work proceeds.

### Gate 1 — non-destructive baseline
At an approved host, record labels, construction, dimensions, mass, condition and photographs. Ask whether electroluminescence, thermography or PV performance testing is in scope. NISE lists these PV services, but acceptance must be confirmed. These are not silver assays.

### Gate 2 — controlled mechanical/thermal separation
Only a qualified facility may perform disassembly, hot-knife/roller comparison, liberation, size reduction or dust capture. The host selects procedures and safeguards. Record sample genealogy, input/output masses, operation ID, host-reported conditions, energy/time, downtime and each output. Compare matched material and retain all residues.

### Gate 3 — characterization and assay
Agree matrix acceptance and preparation with the lab. Define whether the question is bulk Ag in representative solid feed, Ag by size fraction, or exploratory surface mapping. Ask for particle-size limits, minimum mass, validated digestion completeness, blanks/duplicates/reference or matrix-spike QA/QC, detection limits, uncertainty, elements, cost and waste route.

IIT Delhi CRF ICP-MS lists 100–500 mg as generic solid-quantity guidance, says preparation is normally the user's responsibility, urges prior discussion and says unapproved submissions will not be entertained. This does not establish PV-matrix acceptance. Its 2026 charge list shows ₹1,000 + 18% GST per external-academic solid ICP-MS run; preparation, QA/QC, shipping and repeat charges require confirmation. SEM-EDS may help morphology/mapping, not representative bulk Ag assay.

### Gate 4 — route comparison
Compare matched, characterized feed: mechanical liberation plus dry size classification; conditional ECS if the actual particle mechanism justifies it; and flotation only on host-approved prepared feed. For every stream report mass, moisture, size, measured Ag grade, Ag mass, recovery, enrichment and uncertainty. Mass closure and Ag closure are separate. A silver-bearing concentrate is not purified silver.

## Stop conditions

No physical validation without ownership confirmation, qualified supervisor, accepted facility, evaluated module condition, written preparation approval, complete waste route, representative assay/QA plan and stable sample IDs. Do not mail CRF samples without operator approval.
