# AGASTYA evidence review — 2026-10-11

The reviewed evidence supports a transparent accounting framework, not an AGASTYA recovery-performance claim. Published dry size classification has feed-specific Ag-partition results; electrostatic PV research reports Ag co-concentration but no accessible mass-based Ag recovery; bench flotation reports direct Ag recovery on prepared cell feed; and leaching studies report extraction on study-specific material. These endpoints and assays are not directly comparable.

The next AGASTYA-specific study should weigh and assay each stream for a defined sample before predicting Stage 3 performance. The detailed evidence records, route comparison, published-data reproduction and limitations are in Agastya Physics Simulation/Research Phase B-C.
