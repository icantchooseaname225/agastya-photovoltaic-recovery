# NCSC compliance checklist — 2026–27 planning state

This is not a compliance approval. The DST 2026–27 Activity Guidebook is listed on the DST reports page as a draft. Confirm final/local instructions with the event coordinator.

| Requirement or decision | Current evidence | Status | Student action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current NCSC guide | DST PDF located; reports listing labels the 31 March 2026 entry draft | NEEDS REVIEW | Ask coordinator whether this is the applicable final guide |
| Project-book observations | Guidebook says dated, real-time handwritten observations/data are central; printed raw data or rewriting into another book is not accepted | NEEDS REVIEW | Use original project book; do not backfill missing entries |
| Abstract | Guidebook suggests 150–250 words | CONFIRM WITH ORGANISER | Confirm local form and word count; write only from real student work |
| Presentation | Guidebook says practise a concise 3–5 minute explanation | CONFIRM WITH ORGANISER | Treat this as practice guidance, not an event speaking limit |
| Theme | DST's 2025–26 annual report identifies the 2026–27 focal theme as “Science and Innovation for Sustainability”; the activity guide PDF is available, while a separate DST reports-listing entry labels the guidebook draft | NEEDS REVIEW | Confirm the applicable theme/sub-theme and current local pack with the coordinator |
| Local problem/team/guide/field work | DST describes neighbourhood problem selection and student team/guide inquiry | BLOCKED | Identify actual team/guide and student-collected local evidence |
| Category and eligibility | Student/event details not provided | BLOCKED | Confirm with coordinator |
| Report format, signatures and deadlines | No local form or event schedule recorded | BLOCKED | Obtain the current local pack |
| Poster/display requirement, dimensions and count | Not verified | BLOCKED | Ask before choosing A1/A4, copies or mounting |
| Speaking and question time | Only practice guidance found | BLOCKED | Confirm actual slot and question time |
| Digital presentation/device rules | Not verified | BLOCKED | Confirm format, connectivity and backup-device expectations |
| Working model and safety restrictions | Not verified | BLOCKED | Confirm whether a model is expected/allowed and its limits |
| Samples, surveys, interviews, lab/photos | No local approvals or records | BLOCKED | Obtain permissions and adult/facility oversight before collecting or displaying |
