# AGASTYA judge question bank

Answers are intentionally bounded to what the project records support.

## 1. What is AGASTYA trying to solve?

It is a research concept for traceable recovery of materials from end-of-life crystalline-silicon solar panels, with silver as one target. The present work defines evidence and measurement needs; it does not demonstrate silver recovery.

**Support:** AGASTYA project audit and Phase B/C report.  
**Limit:** No local waste survey or physical trial is documented.  
**Follow-up:** Which local end-of-life PV problem will the student team investigate?

## 2. What is your research question?

For a defined feed, what evidence is needed to select and validate the next step toward silver removal while keeping the mass and silver balances traceable?

**Support:** Phase B/C proposal.  
**Limit:** A local sample and actual assay are still needed.

## 3. What are the four stages?

Stage 1 inspection/routing; Stage 2 manual mechanical preparation; Stage 3 thermal treatment/delamination; Stage 4 silver removal/recovery, with the method and product form open. Sampling, assay and mass accounting validate all four stages.

**Support:** Architecture crosswalk.  
**Limit:** This is the current design charter, not validated equipment.

## 4. Did AGASTYA recover silver?

No. There are no documented AGASTYA physical samples, assays or recovery results.

**Support:** Inventory, logbook and readiness audit.  
**Limit:** Published results belong to other studies.

## 5. What does 97.55% mean?

It is the tap-water rougher silver recovery reported by Saffarian et al. for prepared, delaminated and ground PV-cell feed, with a reported upgrade of 32.2×. It is not AGASTYA's recovery.

**Support:** Saffarian et al. 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.mineng.2026.110189.  
**Limit:** Bench-scale, reagent-assisted, different feed.

## 6. What evidence supports size classification?

Sim et al. report category-specific Ag partitions to their finest size class; Juyo Salazar et al. report 94.12% of Ag in a <0.25 mm fraction in their study. These results motivate a test, but are not a universal recovery.

**Support:** DOI 10.1016/j.wasman.2022.11.023; DOI 10.1016/j.susmat.2024.e01047.  
**Limit:** Different feed categories, screen cuts and study methods.

## 7. Why keep electrostatic separation?

Electrostatic separation remains a literature comparator from the earlier route study. The current Stage 3 is thermal delamination; electrostatic separation is not the selected Stage 4 silver-removal route.

**Support:** de Souza & Veit 2023, DOI 10.1016/j.cec.2023.100027; retained as route-comparison literature.  
**Limit:** Co-concentration is not Ag selectivity.

## 8. Why not use silicon recovery as silver recovery?

They are different elements with different particle associations. A silicon recovery percentage cannot substitute for an Ag assay and Ag mass balance.

**Support:** Li et al. 2023, DOI 10.1016/j.jclepro.2023.137908.  
**Limit:** That study's target metric is silicon.

## 9. What is the difference between grade and recovery?

Grade is the fraction of the product mass that is silver. Recovery is the fraction of feed silver that reaches the target product. A small product may have high grade but low recovery, or vice versa.

**Support:** Phase B/C accounting definitions.  
**Limit:** Both require weighed streams and assays.

## 10. What did the simulation calculate?

Historical model work contains 200,080 virtual scenario/time-history rows and later thermal-model studies. They are calculated/simulated records, not experiments; no current Stage 4 silver-removal solver result is documented.

**Support:** Day 23 logbook and model metadata.  
**Limit:** No electrostatic field, DEM or chemistry solver ran.

## 11. Which programmes did you actually use?

CPython 3.14.4 for evidence and accounting scripts, NumPy 2.5.3 in the earlier thermal simulation, and PowerShell for orchestration. PptxGenJS generated the slides; ReportLab generated the posters. Document software is not a physics solver.

**Support:** Software execution audit.  
**Limit:** No Ansys, SimScale, chemistry solver or assay instrument was run.

## 12. Why did you not run a physics engine?

The input geometry, particle size/shape, conductivity, moisture, charge-to-mass, feed rate and validation assays are missing. A field solve alone would not predict Ag recovery.

**Support:** Electrostatic feasibility report.  
**Limit:** This is a readiness assessment, not a claim that those tools are unavailable everywhere.

## 13. What is the next experiment?

First complete the written Stage 3/Stage 4 interface and confirm a traceable sample plus qualified facility. Only then consider a supervised thermal Stage 3 coupon trial; select Stage 4 silver-removal research after feed and required product form are defined. Sampling, assay and mass balance cover every stage.

**Support:** Validation plan.  
**Limit:** Not yet scheduled or authorized.

## 14. What is your environmental benefit?

No lifecycle, cost, energy or emissions benefit has been measured. The future comparison must include energy, dust, residues, transport, reagents, wastewater and receiver acceptance.

**Support:** Sustainability boundary in Phase B/C report.  
**Limit:** Potential impact only.
