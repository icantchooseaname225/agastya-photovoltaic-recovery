# Stage 4 — silver removal and recovery

**Current definition (16 October 2026):** Stage 4 removes/recover silver from Stage 3-derived cell-bearing material. It is not an assay/accounting stage and is not defined as dry classification alone. The owner-corrected four-stage sequence is in [AGASTYA_ARCHITECTURE.md](../../AGASTYA_ARCHITECTURE.md).

The existing files in this folder contain valuable literature and route-feasibility work, but most were written under a prior assumption that Stage 4 was dry pre-concentration or assay/accounting. Treat their method comparisons as evidence about possible feed preparation or silver-removal routes, not as the current stage definition. See the [16 October architecture and route decision](STAGE4_ARCHITECTURE_AND_ROUTE_DECISION_2026-10-16.md).

No Stage 4 method has been selected or physically tested by AGASTYA. The Stage 3 hot-knife reference outputs glass plus a cell/EVA/backsheet laminate; whether that feed requires additional liberation/polymer removal depends on the selected silver-removal method. Sampling, assay, mass balance and chain of custody measure Stage 4 performance but do not replace silver removal.
