## Decision

Stage 4 remains at **design/readiness gate**. There is not enough verified information to authorize a PV feed trial or select a separator model. This is not a conclusion that facilities do not exist; their status has not been checked in the project records available here.

The current research direction remains controlled mechanical liberation and dry size classification for a characterized silver-bearing solid fraction, with ECS only as a conditional secondary comparison if liberated conductive material remains. This route is not validated until an eligible feed and lab access are confirmed. Stage 4 will not recover elemental silver; downstream refining is a separate receiving-partner operation.

## Design-basis separation from chemical refining

A 2026 study reports localized electrolyte-jet recovery from end-of-life silicon cells using circulating 12 wt% nitric acid at 5 V: 90.2% surface-silver dissolution in 7 minutes and >95% separation after 30 minutes. This is a downstream chemical/electrolyte operation, not evidence for dry Stage 4 separation. It may inform what a qualified external refiner could investigate, but it does not alter Agastya's no-on-site-chemistry boundary. See [Zhang et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.seppur.2025.136440).
