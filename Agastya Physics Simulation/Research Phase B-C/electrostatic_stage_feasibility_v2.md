# Stage 3 electrostatic feasibility audit

## Evidence and current status

A 2023 PV-scrap roll-separation study reports co-concentration of Ag, Al and Si at study-specific conditions (abstract reports 38 kV and 75 rpm), but accessible text lacks mass-weighted Ag recovery, product grade and particle charge distributions. A separate electrostatic paper reports silicon results, not silver results. This supports a candidate hypothesis, not a validated AGASTYA stage.

CPython generated accounting and evidence outputs. The existing thermal model is a five-node lumped thermal RC model using explicit forward Euler. Browser and Wokwi content are interface/control concepts. No FEM, DEM, CFD, electrostatic field, chemistry or assay engine was run.

## Inputs needed for a useful model

1. Dimensioned electrode geometry, voltage, polarity, clearances and boundary conditions.
2. Representative particle size, shape, moisture, conductivity and surface-condition distributions.
3. Measured charge-to-mass distributions and charging/decay behavior by material class.
4. Feed rate, airflow and operating environment.
5. Calibrated trajectories and weighed, assayed outputs for validation.

A field solution describes a chosen field and boundary conditions; it does not by itself predict silver recovery. Keep Stage 3 as a research candidate. Any physical test requires a supervised facility, electrical and dust controls, representative specimens and qualified assays. No setpoint or yield forecast is recommended.
