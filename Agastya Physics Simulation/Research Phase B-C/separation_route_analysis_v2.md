# Separation-route analysis

| Route | Evidence in reviewed studies | Meaning of result | AGASTYA implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crush, sieve and classify | Sim et al. 2022 report up to 70% and 80% of feed Ag in F4 for two distinct categories; Juyo Salazar et al. 2024 report 94.12% of Ag in <0.25 mm fines. | Study-specific Ag partition by size; not grade or universal recovery. | Best lower-complexity Stage 2 comparator. Measure stream mass and assay first. |
| Electrostatic roll | de Souza & Veit 2023 report Ag, Al and Si co-concentration; accessible abstract has no mass-based Ag recovery or grade. Li et al. report Si results, not Ag. | Co-concentration is not silver selectivity. | Candidate only; no AGASTYA performance claim. |
| Ag-focused flotation | Saffarian et al. 2026 report 97.55 ± 0.03% recovery and 32.2 ± 2.3× upgrade for a tap-water rougher test; cleaner example reports 46.7 ± 1.5 wt% Ag at 86.5 ± 2.8% recovery. | Direct assay-based result on prepared, ground cell feed with reagents. | Strong direct comparator, not transferable to whole panels or AGASTYA feed. |
| Leaching / chemical recovery | Dias et al. report 94% without and 92% with pyrolysis; Mwase et al. report about 98% recovery and >99.9% product purity from milled cell feed. | Different feed and endpoint; not comparable as one ranking. | Specialist facility, reagent and effluent controls required; not a student demonstration. |
| Thermal delamination | Coupon and model studies report separation/morphology, not silver recovery. | Pretreatment result, not Ag beneficiation. | Optional feed preparation, not a recovery stage result. |

## Conclusion

A universal route ranking is not justified because feed basis, preparation, endpoint and assay method differ. The next AGASTYA-specific study is a supervised size-partition test with defined feed, weighed streams and qualified Ag assays. It can determine whether Stage 2 alone produces an Ag-bearing fraction before Stage 3 is evaluated.

Current charter: Stage 1 disassembly/routing; Stage 2 mechanical liberation/classification; Stage 3 electrostatic candidate; Stage 4 assays/accounting. Thermal delamination is optional pretreatment. Earlier numbering is cross-walked in Research Baseline/ARCHITECTURE_CROSSWALK_2026-10-10.md.
