# AGASTYA validation plan

This is a plan, not a record of completed tests.

## Gate 0 — authority and identity

Confirm a supervised facility, qualified operator, approvals and safety controls. Record sample origin, technology, condition, sample ID and chain of custody. Define permission and disposal.

## Gate 1 — baseline inventory

Weigh and photograph the input and each Stage 1 output using calibrated equipment. Select a qualified laboratory and matrix-appropriate assay method with blanks, certified reference material, duplicates, detection limits and raw instrument records.

## Gate 2 — Stage 2 size partition

Define sieve apertures before work. Characterize particle size and shape. Use independently prepared replicate portions; choose sample mass with the assay laboratory after a representativity study. Weigh all size fractions, dust capture, retained material and losses. Assay each stream and calculate mass split, Ag split, residual and uncertainty.

## Gate 3 — Stage 3 candidate

Pre-register conditions and controls. Compare matched feed with no-separation and mechanical baselines. Record geometry, voltage, polarity, roll speed, feed rate, humidity, moisture, charge-to-mass, size distribution and dust capture. Do not substitute solver predictions for measured recovery.

## Gate 4 — Stage 4 accounting

Report mass yield, Ag grade, Ag recovery and balances separately. Preserve sample IDs, chain of custody, raw instrument files, QA/QC and failed observations. Never force closure.

Stop if sample identity, safety controls or assay QA/QC fail, or if a result depends on an unmeasured assumption. Keep this model a design aid until the gates are completed.
