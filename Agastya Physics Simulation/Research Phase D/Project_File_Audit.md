## Contradictions and disposition

1. **Stage numbering:** the current crosswalk controls this research: Stage 3 is electrostatic; the thesis and older thermal model call hot-knife heating Stage 3. Preserve legacy files and labels; do not relabel old rows.
2. **Stage 4:** current Stage 4 is representative sampling, assay, and accounting. The thesis calls Stage 4 an eddy-current separator. The current project does not demonstrate downstream metallic-silver refining.
3. **Silver basis:** the 2022 review-derived 300 ppm composition scenario is not a measured AGASTYA feed. The 2016 Dias publisher abstract states 600 g/t; 630 g/t appears in a secondary excerpt and remains unresolved. They are not a pooled range.
4. **Thermal energy:** the workbook's whole-EVA sensible-heat calculation assumes all EVA reaches a target temperature and adds a 2.2 multiplier. The five-node RC model represents a separate assumed local heat-transfer problem. Neither validates the roller/EVA removal claim or supplies current-stage energy data.
5. **Electrostatic evidence:** the older register emphasizes a 2023 study that co-concentrates Ag, Al, and Si but lacks an accessible Ag mass balance. A 2019 primary paper reports 94.37% “silver separation efficiency,” but the accessible abstract does not define that metric or show a complete balance. Both remain study-specific.
