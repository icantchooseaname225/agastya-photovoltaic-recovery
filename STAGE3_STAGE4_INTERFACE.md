**Authority:** current [four-stage architecture](AGASTYA_ARCHITECTURE.md), [Stage 3 reassessment](Build%20Files%20and%20Steps/Stage%203/STAGE3_ROLLER_AND_LIBERATION_PLACEMENT_REASSESSMENT_2026-11-19.md), and [Stage 4 reassessment](Build%20Files%20and%20Steps/Stage%204/STAGE4_ARCHITECTURE_REASSESSMENT_2026-11-19.md).

## Provisional process boundary

1. **Stage 2 → Stage 3:** automated mechanical disassembly is intended to supply an identified, electrically isolated, supported crystalline-silicon module. The robotic arm is a design direction; no AGASTYA arm or disassembly trial exists.
2. **Stage 3 operation:** hot-knife glass separation followed by the foam-roller concept. The roller's function, material and operating parameters remain unspecified. Existing thermal simulations cover a layered conduction model, not the roller, peel/fracture, or liberation.
3. **Expected Stage 3 principal outputs:** a separated front-glass stream and cell/EVA/backsheet laminate. These are expected material classes, not AGASTYA measured outputs. The laminate is not assumed EVA-free, cell-only, silver-liberated, or suitable for flotation without further preparation.
4. **Post-thermal liberation option:** test a contained, modular mechanical-liberation unit directly after hot-knife/roller separation. Published industrial process descriptions support that process order, but do not establish a performance or cost benefit for AGASTYA. Keep the module bypassable until output quality, dust, contamination, energy and whole-line effects are measured.
5. **Stage 4 feed and function:** accept the characterized Stage 3 output, with any required comminution or liberation explicitly specified. Compare dry size classification and silver-selective flotation on compatible feed. Conventional eddy-current separation is conditional, not a default. The final product may be a silver-bearing fraction, concentrate, solution or solid; it is not automatically purified silver.

## Placement decision

The next layout should reserve space and a sealed material-transfer interface for liberation immediately after the Stage 3 glass/laminate split. This is a physical-layout proposal. The project should not permanently move liberation into the formal Stage 3 boundary or remove Stage 4 preparation capability until matched tests show that the feed is acceptable and whole-line handling, dust, contamination, energy, maintenance and silver retention are understood.

## Evidence boundary

- IEA PVPS reports an industrial sequence of hot-knife treatment followed by laminate shredding and sorting. This is process-order evidence, not an AGASTYA design specification. [IEA PVPS T12-31:2025](https://doi.org/10.69766/XLFG7020).
- Prior hot-knife LCA/model inputs and outputs belong to their stated system boundaries; they are not measured AGASTYA energy or performance.
- Flotation results belong to prepared, delaminated and ground feed in the cited laboratory study. They are not a forecast for AGASTYA feed. [Saffarian et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mineng.2026.110189).

No stage setting, energy/throughput target, output-quality limit, ECS benefit, or silver-recovery rate is established by this interface. Physical work needs a traceable sample, qualified host, approved equipment/safety review, calibrated measurement plan, assay capability and accepted waste/receiver pathway.
