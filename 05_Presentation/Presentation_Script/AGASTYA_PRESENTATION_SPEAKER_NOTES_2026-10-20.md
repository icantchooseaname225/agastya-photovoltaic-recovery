### Slide 7: Thermal delamination is the Stage 3 reference

Stage 3 is thermal treatment/delamination; the guarded hot-knife is a literature-based reference, not a built or validated Agastya machine. IEA PVPS Task 12-25:2023, full report reviewed 20 October 2026, describes pilot-plant hot-knife delamination; its functional unit is 1 kg of framed c-Si module at the installation location, data quality is rated fair, and downstream Cu/Ag recovery is further treatment outside its process boundary. Report: https://iea-pvps.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Report_IEA-PVPS_T12-25-2023_LCA-PV-Recycling-Hot-Knife.pdf. NPC manufacturer page (checked 20 October 2026) claims sheet-glass separation for non-broken modules, about 60 seconds per panel and a 300 C knife; this is a vendor claim, not an Agastya setting or performance result: https://www.npcgroup.net/eng/solarproduct/dismantling. The 1-D five-node RC model is numerical and assumption-based. It does not model fracture, peel force, fumes, safety, actual heater energy or separation quality. No physical sample or trial is recorded.

### Slide 10: Electrostatic evidence is incomplete for silver

Electrostatic PV separation is shown only as a Stage 4 route comparator; it is not the current Stage 3 and no route is selected. de Souza & Veit (2023), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cec.2023.100027, reports Ag/Al/Si co-concentration in prepared PV scrap. The accessible abstract does not provide mass-weighted Ag recovery, product grade or complete product masses. This literature result is not an Agastya result and cannot determine the route without matched feed and qualified facility data.

### Slide 11: A strong result on a different feed

Saffarian et al. (2026), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mineng.2026.110189, report study-specific flotation results on prepared, delaminated and ground PV-cell feed with reagents. These are a Stage 4 literature comparator only, not an Agastya prediction or student procedure. No Agastya feed, trial, assay or recovery is documented.
