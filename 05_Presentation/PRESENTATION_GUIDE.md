## Claims that must stay consistent

Say “proposed” for the four-stage process. Stage 3 is thermal treatment/delamination; Stage 4 is silver removal/recovery, with method, feed preparation and product form still open. Sampling, assay, mass accounting and uncertainty validate all stages. The electrostatic study reports Ag/Al/Si co-concentration but does not provide an accessible complete Ag mass balance. The separate silicon recovery paper does not measure silver recovery. Literature flotation/chemical figures refer to their own prepared feeds and conditions. The 300 ppm scenario has unresolved source-basis provenance; the directly traceable Dias abstract reports 600 g/t, while 630 g/t is still a secondary-source discrepancy. No AGASTYA physical result, silver-removal model, recovered silver, purity, throughput or cost result is available.
