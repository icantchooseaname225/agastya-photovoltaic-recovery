# Start here — AGASTYA

## Current four-stage direction

**Stage 1:** AGASTYA-EYE vision-based triage and traceability.  
**Stage 2:** automated mechanical disassembly using a robotic-arm-centered workcell.  
**Stage 3:** thermal separation using the hot-knife and foam-roller concept.  
**Stage 4:** material liberation, separation, and silver recovery; the route is still open.

The current owner direction and evidence classes are defined in [AGASTYA_ARCHITECTURE.md](AGASTYA_ARCHITECTURE.md). It supersedes the older manual Stage 2 baseline. Historic logbooks, thesis sections, models, and October presentation files remain preserved as historic records.

## Evidence boundary

No AGASTYA physical panel processing, robotic disassembly, thermal/foam-roller trial, Stage 4 separation, silver assay, or recovered-silver result is documented. The 200,080 historical simulation rows are virtual scenarios/time histories, not physical observations. A silver-bearing fraction or concentrate is not purified silver.

Before any physical work, confirm a traceable panel, approved facility and supervision, equipment/safety review, representative sampling and assay plan, and waste/receiver pathway. Do not copy literature settings into an AGASTYA procedure without qualified review.
