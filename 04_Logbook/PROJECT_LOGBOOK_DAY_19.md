# Agastya Project Logbook: Day 19

**Date:** 7 October 2026  
**Record status:** targeted literature comparison. No Stage 4 equipment was operated or purchased, and no PV sample was processed.

## Objective

Research the two Stage 4 routes identified in the prior review: direct comminution and size classification for a silver-bearing hand-off, and delamination for intact-cell recovery.

## Work completed

- Verified the crush-and-sieve route's size cuts in the literature: >5 mm, 1–5 mm, 0.25–1 mm and <0.25 mm. The smallest fraction contained 70–80% of total Ag in the two studied categories; that is Ag distribution, not product purity.
- Reviewed Juyo Salazar et al.'s output fractions: the reported <0.25 mm Ag-bearing cut includes silicon, while the 0.5–2 mm stream contained substantial glass and polymer despite high Cu distribution. Recorded that size cuts are mixed materials and require grade, recovery and receiver checks.
- Compared the silver-fines route with a 2025 mild-crushing route targeting Si-cell-bearing particles. Its >1 mm fraction had 25.36% mass yield and 50.32% cell recovery after 10 seconds; downstream clean-cell recovery still relied on gravity and pyrolysis.
- Reviewed the backsheet hot-knife, heat-assisted back-EVA and laser-assisted back-EVA methods. Clarified that the reported 99.97% in the heat-assisted method is EVA purity, not total module EVA recovery, and that the full sequence still includes a 400 °C treatment for clean glass/silicon.
- Expanded the feed-gap research report with study conditions, output-specific limitations, and direct comparison between the two target products.

## Recommendation

For a mixed Ag-bearing solid destined for a central refiner, continue evaluating contained comminution and multi-cut sieving directly on the actual Stage 3 laminate; do not add full EVA peeling as an assumed prerequisite. Require assay and mass closure because published high Ag distribution does not equal high product grade. For intact cells, retain delamination as an external partner route; current literature does not justify designing a complete mobile EVA-removal station.

Keep the ECS as a conditional step only if the measured size fractions reveal a liberated conductor stream and it adds assay-confirmed benefit. No machine price or throughput estimate is justified without feed, facility access, dust controls, assay service and receiver criteria.

Ask a supervised recycler or university lab whether it can accept Agastya's traceable Stage 3 laminate for a contained size-fraction study and assay. In parallel, request the exact interface-removal boundary and output specification for any partner cell/EVA delamination service.
