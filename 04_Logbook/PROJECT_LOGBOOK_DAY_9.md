# Agastya Project Logbook: Day 9

**Date:** 3 October 2026  
**Record status:** literature review and research-plan revision only. No Stage 4 separator, PV feed preparation, or material recovery experiment is claimed.

## Objective

Read the thesis's Stage 4 proposal and check whether its eddy-current-separation output claim is supported by PV-recycling literature.

## Work completed

- Reviewed the thesis's proposed eddy-current separator and optional electrostatic polishing step.
- Compared PV-waste treatment evidence with experimental ECS research on non-ferrous metal separation and particle-size effects.
- Recorded a limitation in the thesis claim: literature supports ECS as a candidate for separating liberated non-ferrous conductive particles from nonconductors, but the sources reviewed do not show an ECS recovering printed silver from silicon cell fragments.
- Identified the missing Stage 3-to-Stage 4 feed definition. The Stage 3 hot-knife baseline outputs a cell/EVA/backsheet laminate; the proposed heated-roller EVA removal has not been validated.
- Defined Stage 4 pilot measurements: feed and product masses, size class, operating conditions, mass closure, product grade, target-metal recovery, and specific energy. Grade and recovery require suitable composition analysis; mass and visual appearance alone do not establish purity.
- Added `rpm` as an EYE measurement unit so a measured Stage 4 rotor speed can be recorded with the panel passport.

## Findings and limits

Stage 4 should initially be described as an investigation of dry, size-classified, liberated feed for coarse non-ferrous metal enrichment. It should not claim pure silver, pure silicon, or reusable cells. Electrostatic separation remains a possible later comparison for fine, prepared PV particles and requires separate equipment and supervision.

Sources, the evidence table, limitations, and phased pilot plan are in `../Build Files and Steps/Stage 4/STAGE4_LITERATURE_REVIEW.md`. No separator specification, price, hardware build, or recovery result is established by this desk review.
