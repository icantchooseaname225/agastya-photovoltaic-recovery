# Agastya Project Logbook: Day 17

**Date:** 7 October 2026  
**Record status:** Stage 4 desk research and model critique. No equipment was purchased or operated; no PV feed was processed and no performance test was conducted.

## Objective

Stress-test the Stage 4 eddy-current separator and optional electrostatic-polishing concept against Agastya's documented process outputs, then compare alternatives.

## Work completed

- Reviewed the current Stage 4 roadmap, feasibility review, literature review and the Stage 3-to-Stage 4 material boundary.
- Identified the most serious model gap: the present Stage 3 evidence produces a cell/EVA/backsheet laminate, not liberated conductive particles. A response from a silver-bearing silicon composite in an eddy-current field would not by itself demonstrate silver enrichment.
- Documented missing feed acceptance, liberation, receiver specification, assay, dust control, whole-line capacity and operating-cost inputs.
- Compared controlled liberation and multi-cut sieving, gas-solid fluidized-bed separation, corona electrostatic separation, fixed-frequency EDX, high-voltage pulse liberation, froth flotation, conventional ECS and a partner-recycler hand-off.
- Clarified that Juyo Salazar et al.'s reported 94.12% Ag in the <0.25 mm fraction describes Ag distribution/recovery to that fraction, not a 94.12 wt% Ag product.
- Added a dedicated model critique and alternatives review; updated the roadmap and feasibility review and linked the new document in the project index.

## Decision

Do not build or purchase an ECS based on the thesis drawing. Keep Stage 4's provisional goal as assay-verified pre-concentration of a defined mixed fraction for a named receiver. If safe feed, supervised access, dust controls, assay and acceptance criteria can be confirmed, study controlled liberation plus multi-cut size classification first and compare one separator only on equivalent feed. Keep ECS conditional on a liberated coarse conductor fraction and measured assay benefit.

If those gates cannot be met, hand the Stage 3 laminate to a qualified recycler and keep Agastya focused on mobile pre-processing and traceability. No total Stage 4 cost, recovery rate or full-line throughput is supportable yet. “No process chemicals” must not be described as “no consumables”; include dust filters, sample containers, screen cleaning, PPE and maintenance when quoting costs.

## Next step

Verify actual feed and partner-lab/receiver access, and request the receiver's acceptance specification and assay requirements. Update the material-flow diagram only after confirming the real output at the Stage 3 boundary.
