# Agastya Project Logbook: Day 15

**Date:** 7 October 2026  
**Record status:** literature review and calculations only. No PV material was processed, no facility contacted, and no equipment or service purchased.

## Objective

Extend the Stage 4 literature review with recent dry physical separation evidence and decide what it implies for Agastya's mobile, no-on-site-chemistry design.

## Work completed

- Revisited the 2024 streamlined comminution/sieving study. It reports 94.12% of Ag in the <0.25 mm fraction and 99.37% of Cu in a 0.5–2.00 mm cut; that Cu cut also contained 76.85% glass and 64.04% polymer.
- Revisited the 2026 Montanuniversität Leoben MSc thesis on rod-mill glass abrasion, hammer-mill size reduction and Hamos Corona-drum electrostatic sorting. The fine 0.1–0.71 mm fraction reported >80% recovery across glass, silicon and metallic fractions with about 53% silicon grade at a selected condition; the coarser cut needed separate optimization.
- Added a mass-normalized calculation for the 2024 size-classification result: of 100 feed-silver units, 94.12 report to the <0.25 mm cut. Product grade and product mass cannot be calculated from this distribution result without the fraction mass yield and assay details.
- Updated the Stage 4 method comparison, literature review and roadmap to separate size distribution, grade and recovery and to state evidence class/technology limits.
- Reconfirmed the working route: characterize the real Stage 3 output first; then compare size-classified fractions on supervised, assay-supported feed. Treat corona electrostatics as a specialized lab alternative and ECS as conditional on eligible liberated particles.

## Decision and limits

The newer studies strengthen size-classification as a first dry pre-concentration candidate, but do not establish an Agastya screen size, throughput, silver grade, saleable product or cost. The 2026 electrostatic result is a master's thesis using specialized high-voltage equipment, so it is a research comparator, not a school build plan. Stage 3 feed, lab access, assay and refiner acceptance remain unverified.
