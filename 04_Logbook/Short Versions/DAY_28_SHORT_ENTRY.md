# Day 28 — Stage 3 feasibility and proposed Stage 4 direction

**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Status:** AI-assisted digital research draft for owner review. No physical trial, assay, simulation or solver run.  
**Full entry:** [PROJECT_LOGBOOK_DAY_28.md](../PROJECT_LOGBOOK_DAY_28.md)

**Objective:** Review the current stage handoff, investigate Stage 3 delamination limits, and assess an optional two-step Stage 4 proposal.

**Findings:** Current architecture is Stage 1 inspection/routing; Stage 2 manual frame/J-box/cable removal; Stage 3 guarded hot-knife glass removal yielding glass plus cell/EVA/backsheet laminate; Stage 4 silver removal/recovery with route open. The thesis's automated Stage 2, EVA-free Stage 3 output and eddy-current Stage 4 are historical concepts, not validated current design. EYE is documented as advisory/dry-run with uncertain and unsupported cases routed for review; field performance is unverified. Stage 2 has no measured silver liberation or PSD, so preserve intact laminate for Stage 3.

Thermal transfer, interface temperature, EVA/backsheet residue, peel damage, feed variability and silver accounting remain unmeasured. Published hot-knife, oven-peel, crushing/sieving, electrostatic and flotation results are feed-specific. Proposed Stage 4A is optional and should omit eddy-current separation unless a measured liberated Cu/Al stream justifies it. Proposed Stage 4B is Stage 4 mechanical liberation, size classification and dry corona electrostatic concentration; it may produce an Ag-bearing concentrate, not silver metal. Flotation has stronger Ag-specific evidence but uses reagents and water.

**Decision status:** Proposal only; no canonical architecture or thesis changed. First obtain traceable feed, supervised coupon measurements, validated Ag/Cu/Al assays, particle-size/liberation data and a mass balance. See the full entry for risks, literature limits and next actions.
