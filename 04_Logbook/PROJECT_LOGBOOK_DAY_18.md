# Agastya Project Logbook: Day 18

**Date:** 7 October 2026  
**Record status:** literature review and design recommendation only. No feed was processed, no equipment was operated or purchased, and no performance or cost result was measured.

## Objective

Research how to repair the mismatch between Stage 3's cell/EVA/backsheet laminate and the Stage 4 separator concept, and evaluate the proposed controlled comminution and size-classification recommendation.

## Work completed

- Reviewed the Stage 3 build plan and Stage 4 feed requirements to confirm that the hot-knife output is a laminate, not EVA-free cells or free conductive particles.
- Compared direct controlled comminution plus multi-cut size classification with mechanical/thermal backsheet removal, heat-assisted back-EVA separation, laser-assisted peeling, and specialist partner delamination.
- Rechecked the PV crush-and-sieve evidence: published Ag shares in fine fractions describe how much total Ag partitions to a fraction, not the fraction's Ag weight percentage. Compared it with mild-crushing results for a separate cell-bearing coarse fraction, which had lower cell recovery and still required pyrolysis downstream.
- Reviewed IEA PVPS Task 12's 2025 description of EVERPV pilot routes that mechanically remove the backsheet and then separate cell+EVA material; recorded this as a partner-processing lead, not an available Agastya supplier or validated design.
- Created a feed-gap research report and updated the Stage 4 roadmap, feed requirements, feasibility review, literature review and project index.

## Recommendation

Separate two targets: a mixed Ag-bearing hand-off and intact/EVA-free cells. For the first target, complete EVA peeling need not be the baseline assumption. The next research route is supervised, contained comminution and multi-cut classification directly on traceable Stage 3 laminate, then assay and mass-balance every fraction. This route may enrich Ag in fines, but does not recover pure silver or prove the fine fraction is high-grade.

If intact cells are required, pursue hot-knife backsheet removal and heat-assisted or laser debonding as partner-lab options. Current literature does not establish a simple complete mobile delamination design; one reported heat-assisted sequence includes a 400 °C treatment for clean glass/silicon recovery. Keep the thesis ECS conditional and do not build it unless a liberated eligible stream and assay-confirmed benefit are demonstrated.

## Next step

Ask a supervised lab/recycler whether it can accept the actual Stage 3 laminate for contained size classification and assay. In parallel, ask a qualified recycler about its backsheet and cell/EVA separation route and sample acceptance. Request the receiver specification before fixing target size cuts or estimating costs. No physical trial or purchase is justified until feed, facility controls, assay and acceptance requirements are confirmed.
