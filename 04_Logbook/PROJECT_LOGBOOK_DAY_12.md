## Decisions and findings

- Do not specify or build the thesis's ECS until feed state, particle size, liberation and target product are defined. Current Stage 3 glass-separated laminate is not an ECS-ready particle feed.
- Keep ECS for a conditional coarse liberated-conductor comparison; it cannot be claimed to extract printed silver from silicon.
- If an appropriately supervised lab and eligible feed are available, use size classification as the first physical comparison, then select a follow-on method based on whether the target is silver-rich fines, silicon-rich powder or coarse conductors.
- Flotation is a strong recent silver-concentration benchmark, but it falls outside Agastya's current no-on-site-chemistry pathway and would require an external qualified lab.
- High-voltage pulse crushing and electrostatic separation remain literature-only options until specialized supervised equipment is available.
- Equipment cost, Agastya energy cost, product yield and profitability remain uncalculated because no equipment quote, measured feed assay, measured run energy, tariff or buyer specification is available.
